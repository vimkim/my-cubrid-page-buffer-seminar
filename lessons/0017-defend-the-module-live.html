01 · Your job as presenter

Teach a model; route the catalog

Your team does not need a recitation of every function. They need a model that predicts behavior, a source route that verifies strong claims, and an honest boundary when evidence stops.

Present one page journey. Use the document set to expand, diagnose, change, verify, or qualify it.

Keep the pinned revision visible. If someone asks about another branch, say what must be re-traced instead of silently carrying the claim forward.

02 · Six moves

Tell the same causal story every time

MoveClaim to deliverQuestion it answers
1 · Objects and boundaryVPID → BCB ↔ frame → PAGE_PTR; the pointer is a temporary borrowed view.“What exactly did fix return?”
2 · Acquire and releaseHit and miss converge on latch grant plus global and per-thread fix debt; one success needs one release.“Why can a hit wait, and what prevents reuse?”
3 · Caller correctnessThe caller still owns logical locking, validation, mutation, logging, page LSA, dirtying, and all-exit cleanup.“Why is a WRITE latch insufficient?”
4 · Flush generationCopy G, preserve concurrent G+1, force WAL before page propagation, and keep failure retryable.“How can DIRTY and FLUSHING both be true?”
5 · Replace safelyEligibility is a hard protected predicate; policy chooses only among eligible BCBs; identity may change only after safe detachment.“Why is fcnt==0 insufficient?”
6 · Change with evidenceName owner, state, guard, invariant, negative path, caller impact, and risk-matched proof level.“Is this source concern a verified defect?”

04 · Hard-question pattern

Answer in five moves, then stop

  1. Short answer: one direct sentence.
  2. Owner/state/guard: identify who changes what under which protection.
  3. Invariant and consequence: state what must remain true and what breaks otherwise.
  4. Evidence level: contract, verified mechanism, policy, inference, runtime observation, or historical evidence.
  5. Route: point to the Canonical explanation, source range, registry row, or verification seam.
ChallengeFirst sentenceExpansion route
“If it is resident, why can fix block?”Residency and latch grant are independent axes.Acquisition concurrency and latch queue.
“Can one BCB belong to two pages?”One stable slot has one current VPID, but may be reused serially after protected detachment.Replacement safety and identity timeline.
“Statistics show zero; can we deallocate?”An approximate diagnostic snapshot cannot authorize a state transition.Specialized interfaces, then protected owner path.
“Is VS-12 a real bug?”The pinned source proves an ordering concern; reachability, surviving state, impact, and current status remain separate.Registry, failure-proof ledger, target injection.
05 · Honest uncertainty

“I do not know yet” can be a maintainer-quality answer

Do not guess a fairness bound, current-branch status, metric meaning, or failure consequence. Replace a vague unknown with a bounded next step:

“The pinned source establishes X. It does not establish Y. Y is owned by this registry row or mechanism, and this source trace, controlled schedule, fault injection, pressure test, or crash boundary would answer it.”

This is not evasion. It shows that you understand the difference between the model, the implementation revision, and the evidence needed for a stronger statement.

06 · Rehearsal loop

Practice delivery, navigation, then resistance

  1. Deliver the six moves closed-book. Record or transcribe the run without repairing it midstream.
  2. Mark every missing transition, undefined term, unsupported claim, and broken route.
  3. Repeat with the guide available and navigate one claim to its owner page and pinned source.
  4. Answer four shuffled counterexamples without changing the core spine.
  5. Rehearse with one storage, logging/recovery, or access-method teammate; record their question and your corrected answer.

Once your actual duration and audience mix are known, compress detail inside each move; do not reorder the causal spine.

07 · Presentation gate

Deliver the opening and survive one challenge

Prompt

Give your closed-book opening through all six moves. Then answer one challenge: a resident hit blocks; DIRTY and FLUSHING are both set; a BCB's VPID differs later; a statistic is zero; or a VS-* row looks true.

Your task: keep one causal narrative, then answer the challenge using short answer → owner/state/guard → invariant/consequence → evidence level → route.

Paste the transcript into the chat. I will score only demonstrated delivery, repair the narrowest gap, and preserve your strongest wording for the next rehearsal.
