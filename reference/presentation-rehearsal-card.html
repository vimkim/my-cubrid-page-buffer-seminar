Challenge deck

  • A resident hit blocks.
  • Two threads hold one BCB.
  • DIRTY and FLUSHING are both set.
  • fcnt==0, but victimization is unsafe.
  • The same BCB address has another VPID.
  • An approximate count is zero.
  • A VS-* source concern looks obvious.
