Follow the same frame through a miss, first placement, and another access. Predict each transition before opening its explanation. The first sequence ends with an unchanged LRU1 position; continue through scan-driven cooling, cross-context migration, and a fully occupied pool's first victim-list choice.

0 · A window into a larger pool

Constructed trace, not a runtime observation. Rules are pinned to CUBRID f799e05d77d5300c6ea5753b4a6cc7caee6d8912. Textbook exact LRU puts each accessed item at the most-recent end. Here, inspect the actual fix and final-unfix branches before predicting movement.
