LRU worked example: admission through policy defense

Follow the same frame through a miss, first placement, and another access. Predict each transition before opening its explanation. Continue through scan-driven cooling, cross-context migration, list selection, protected frame reuse, and concurrent progress. Finish by defending a hypothetical immediate-promotion policy. Each alternative restarts from its named snapshot; do not carry state between branches.

0 · A window into a larger pool

Constructed trace, not a runtime observation. Rules are pinned to CUBRID f799e05d77d5300c6ea5753b4a6cc7caee6d8912. Textbook exact LRU puts each accessed item at the most-recent end. Here, inspect the actual fix and final-unfix branches before predicting movement.

Server mode, num_buffers = 32768; shared_lists = 32; private_lists = 4 (explicit private-chain count). Context A carries private-local p = 0, full list 32; context B carries p = 1, full list 33. Policy proposal · define “immediate” before comparing

Hypothetical alternative, not pinned behavior: on every successful ordinary resident OLD_PAGE READ or WRITE fix, including nested and fast-path successes, move the already-linked BCB to the top of LRU1 of its current list before returning the page pointer. The trigger is completed latch and holder grant, not hash lookup, a waiting request, or a failed fix. Miss admission, NEW_PAGE/recovery/specialized fetches and same-debt latch promotion are outside this bounded proposal. No engine change or measured improvement is claimed.

Protection assumption: retain the successful fix debt, acquire or retain the BCB mutex, revalidate identity and current membership, then take that list's mutex for unlink/relink and zone accounting. A fast-path success must join this protected path before returning; it cannot keep its existing no-list-lock cost. Never hold two list mutexes together. Audit every grant/return and failure path before implementation: the existing boost helper rejects LRU1 and is not a drop-in patch. Waiting, timeout, cancellation, holder failures and lock ordering still require proof.

Destination and migration: an own-private hit stays in that private list; another context's private hit also promotes within the old list; an already-shared hit stays in that shared list. Keep existing final-unfix rules, including ignore hints, private-to-shared mismatch/hot-and-old migration to shared LRU2 and its selector. Thus B's hit does not move P into B's private list 33; the later zero-count unfix can supersede the earlier LRU1 placement. All examples are ordinary, non-hot, clean, waiter-free events with no hints or intervening events, unless a reset explicitly says otherwise.

Exact hypothetical bookkeeping and its limits

Every qualifying hit unlinks and inserts once, even if already top. Increment tick_list once using the existing top-insertion wrap rule; preserve the BCB's saved tick on same-list promotion. From LRU1, counts stay equal; from LRU2, adjust zone 1; from LRU3, adjust combined zones then zone 1. Preserve boundary pointers, encoded index/zone, candidate counters and hint/queue maintenance. These are proposed obligations, not proof that current helpers are safe at all fix sites. Final-unfix activity sampling remains at its existing location; do not add a hit_age sample at fix. Preserve quota epochs, hot-fix accounting and dormant AOUT. Cross-list insertion still saves the destination's pre-insertion tick. More list ticks can change later age/migration decisions even without elapsed time; thresholds and quota formulas are unchanged but their future inputs need re-derivation. Open the model review and verification plan

Concrete counterexample to a universal benefit: both policies have 20 resident hits and zero misses. Baseline keeps private tick 1002 and F43/R → F42/P → H1 → H2 throughout. The alternative performs 20 protected relinks, alternates the first two frames, and ends with the same order but tick 1022. Counts remain 4/48/50; P/R saved ticks remain 1000/1001; both hit ages stay 10 and lru_hits[32] stays 2. Neither becomes hot within these 20 events. INVALID stays 30,766 and resident stays 2,002. There is no residency gain in this bounded window; extra locking/bookkeeping is structural cost, not a measured slowdown.

Model decision: do not accept an unconditional performance claim. Earlier protection of recently reused LRU2/3 pages could help a phase-changing workload survive later pressure. Conversely, bypassing the LRU2 age gate can promote a scan page's quick second touch and displace another protected node; repeated LRU1 touches add list-mutex traffic without adding residency in the counterexample. These are workload hypotheses. Reset B already shows that earlier motion can produce the same eventual placement and that final unfix can undo the requested LRU1 destination through migration. More ticks can age other pages sooner, and shared-list contention may dominate any reduced misses. Measure the total workload effect.

Safety remains mandatory: one current VPID per resident frame; balanced holder/fcnt debt; one list membership; protected links, zones, counts and hints; no ordinary victim reuse of a fixed, dirty, flushing, reserved or otherwise ineligible candidate; preserve the direct-assignment path's protected validation and revocation; identity/eligibility recheck before detach; WAL and re-dirty obligations unchanged. Promotion is a preference update, never permission to overwrite. Test reservation/reacquisition/revocation and flush progress through the canonical safety/progress routes; no fairness guarantee follows. VS-* names entries in the uncertainty registry; VS-14 and VS-21 remain proof/sampling limits, and the proposal does not repair VS-19/20. AOUT remains dormant.

Instrument successful resident grants and protected list transitions, then verify no promotion on failed/aborted acquisition, correct nested/fast-path debt, same-list shared hits, zero-count migration, zone thresholds, hint/candidate updates and tick wrap. Force a grant-versus-victim scan race, a cross-context unfix race, a quota pass during hits, dirty/re-dirty flushing and reservation revocation. Assert identity, membership, counts, lock order and all-exit cleanup, including injected holder failure. Use standard CMake/ctest and appropriate existing regression suites; confirm the test reaches its intended source branch. This is a proposed verification plan; no native experiment was run here.

Performance plan: compare unchanged baseline and prototype with equal pool size, page size, dataset, warmup, configuration, seed, hardware and build mode. Sweep P/R working-set size, scan length and one-touch/two-touch frequency, private/shared access, dirty ratio and concurrency. Repeat each run and retain raw receipts plus variation.
