Fix-debt ledger card

Count successful grants, attribute them per thread, and repay exactly one debt per unfix.

Pinned: f799e05Global fcntThread holders

One BCB, two ledgers

Grant rule

  • Successful fix: global fcnt + 1.
  • Successful fix: caller holder fix_count + 1.
  • No grant: no debt and no matching unfix.
  • Equal pointer addresses do not merge successful calls.

Release rule

  • One unfix: caller holder − 1.
  • One unfix: global fcnt − 1.
  • Caller holder reaches zero: remove holder.
  • Global reaches zero: ordinary reuse becomes possible only if all other predicates pass.
