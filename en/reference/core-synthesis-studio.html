The eight required moves

Follow one page, then defend the model

1

Module boundary

What does a successful fix guarantee? Which correctness work still belongs to the caller or another dependency?

2

Page journey

Trace VPID → fix → holder/latch → mutation/logging → DIRTY → flush → victim eligibility → BCB reuse.

3

Independent axes

Explain residency, ownership, content-latch, and dirty/flush state. Give one combination that disproves a tempting synonym.

4

Generation reasoning

Explain conceptual G and G+1, distinguish them from page LSA, and interpret all four DIRTY/FLUSHING combinations.

5

Safe replacement

Why is fcnt==0 insufficient? How can one stable BCB slot represent different VPIDs over time without representing two simultaneously?

6

Evidence-bounded concern

Choose VS-11 or VS-12. Separate what pinned source verifies from reachability, surviving state, impact, and current-branch status.

7

Latch wait

Distinguish a request queued on a BCB from a grant recorded in the atomic tuple and the resumed thread's holder. State the fairness boundary.

8

Document routes

Where do you enter to learn, change, debug, locate source/invariants, check evidence status, or rehearse?
