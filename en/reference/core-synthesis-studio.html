Open the explanation

A successful fix returns a borrowed PAGE_PTR after establishing latch ownership and fix accounting. VPID identifies the logical page; BCB and frame hold its resident control state and bytes. The caller still owns logical locking, record validation, logging, page LSA, dirty marking, and cleanup on every exit.

Flush copies a stable generation G while the resident frame may acquire newer changes, labeled G+1 for reasoning. WAL must precede the relevant page propagation. Finishing G does not make concurrent G+1 durable, and unfixed does not mean safe to evict. Protected victim checks must establish eligibility before the stable BCB/frame slot can be rebound to another identity.

A queued latch request is distinct from an atomic grant and the resumed thread's holder completion. This distinction does not establish strict fairness. Likewise, a source-visible VS concern does not alone establish reachability, surviving state, production impact, or behavior on another branch.

The next question determines the evidence route: canonical explanations for the mental model, playbooks for change and diagnosis, source and invariant references for implementation, and the inventory or uncertainty registry for provenance and current status.
