Ownership in one view

EngineReplacement protectionContent protectionCleanup owner
CUBRIDBCB atomic fcntREAD/WRITE latch requested through pgbuf_fix()Per-thread holder; matching pgbuf_unfix()

InnoDBAtomic buf_fix_countBlock S/SX/X latchmtr_t memo releases latch, then fix

A pin or fix protects identity from reuse. A latch or content lock protects bytes from incompatible access.

Replacement in one line

  • CUBRID: choose one private/shared LRU domain, then inspect its cold LRU3 zone.
  • PostgreSQL: move a clock hand and decay bounded usage_count credit.
  • InnoDB: admit disk reads into the old region and scan backward from its tail.
No winner follows from these shapes. Workload, effective capacity, cleaning, and contention still require measurement.
