Mission completion audit

What evidence must exist before the goal can close

  1. Integrated Core retrieval: object map, page journey, independent axes, caller boundary, generation, replacement, evidence, and latch wait.
  2. Pinned source capability: successful fix/release, representative mutation, one generation flush, and safe frame reuse traced in the target source.
  3. Advanced transfer: promotion/ordered access, pressure/progress, redo/lifecycle, specialized interfaces, and failure proof explained through Core invariants.
  4. Operational navigation: one change, symptom, evidence-status, and source/invariant question routed live.
  5. Maintainer review evidence: coherent independent explanation, counterexamples, evidence labels, adversarial defense, and recorded peer feedback.

Course availability proves readiness of the teaching system. Learner-produced artifacts prove readiness of the maintainer.

This matrix is a routing and evidence audit, not a score. Update learner state only from submitted work, corrected misconceptions, source traces, peer-review receipts, or confirmed mission changes.
