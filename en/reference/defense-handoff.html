1 / 6 · Problem and invariant

Remembered candidates are observations across a gap

The BCB array supplies stable management-object storage, but its current VPID and state can change. A candidate record saves both BCB pointer and VPID: the pointer locates the object; identity detects F/P becoming F/Q. A matching VPID cannot prove that P stayed unfixed, cold or free of another flush.

Safety: authorize capture or reuse from current protected state, and never reuse while another caller owns a fix or unpreserved dirty work. Policy: prefer LRU3 and respect the private quota guard. Identity and fixes protect different facts; coldness is not a durability certificate.

Inference: Collecting under the list mutex then releasing it before BCB work bounds list protection. It creates a deliberate validation gap, not a reservation.

2 / 6 · Mechanism and evidence

Submission, completion, offer and receiving are four events

After collection and sorting, lock each BCB and compare VPID, DIRTY and !FLUSHING; reject fixed or hotter candidates, then check WAL. A remembered F/P that now names Q fails identity. Same P with a new fix, new flush or changed zone can fail state checks.

The source explicitly offloads completion under victim pressure. Handoff requires page-flush as producer, an existing post-flush daemon, an allocation waiter and successful queue insertion. Queue full or another failed prerequisite takes local completion. Post-flush locks the BCB, may assign it, then clears FLUSHING and wakes flush waiters even when assignment is rejected. An allocator wake still precedes its own protected receiving check.

Verified mechanism: page_buffer.c:4040–4088 · page_buffer.c:15487–15660 · conditional delegation and fallback.

Narrow removal: Delete only VPID comparison while retaining all current eligibility and common flush guards: F/P may become F/Q and Q may be flushed instead. That establishes wrong selection attribution, not necessarily corruption. Deleting ownership or dirty rechecks is a different counterfactual with a safety consequence.

4 / 6 · Alternatives and costs

Replace revalidation with continuous ownership, at a cost

Teaching alternative: Reserve F before leaving the protected interval, prevent new fixes, and transfer a reservation token with a lifetime guarantee. It can avoid this receiver race, but must coordinate lookup, cancellation, abandoned waiters and teardown. Holding an LRU mutex over I/O would impose storage latency on unrelated list operations. Continuous reservation is not the pinned implementation.

Implemented alternative: Local completion handles unavailable/full handoff without a second thread. It preserves completed-image bookkeeping and flush-waiter wakeups; it need not offer a frame at that moment.

Define L lists, K inspected nodes, C sorted candidates, N possible neighbor span, T counter shards. A selected page-flush pass has structural work O(T + L + K + C log C + C·N). Queue processing adds O(Q + S + W) for Q BCB entries, S stale allocator records and W flush waiters, excluding atomic retries, lock contention and I/O latency.

Q counts entries actually consumed, not queue capacity: a producer can refill during a drain. The fixed/eligible helper also accounts for waiting users, so a raw zero-fix gauge is not a complete authorization predicate.

6 / 6 · Defense and falsifiability

D27 · Can a queue entry become a reuse certificate?

A reviewer proposes carrying a “clean at enqueue” boolean to avoid consumer and receiver checks. VPID is still P. Before consume, P becomes hot and a writer dirties it, then releases its fix. Which checks may be policy, and which omission threatens safety?

Challenge, then reveal the bounded answer

Cold-zone/private-quota checks enforce retention policy. Current dirty and fix/invalid-victim checks protect reuse and ownership. Zero fixes after the writer leaves does not remove its dirty work. An enqueue boolean is stale evidence.

Follow-up: “Recheck everything twice?” Each check protects a different interval. Eliminating one requires a proof that its relevant state cannot change across that interval, not repetition for its own sake.

Discriminating observation: Timestamp enqueue, BCB-lock state, completion, offer publication and protected reception; classify skip counters by predicate. Q07–Q13 and their existing independent traces remain the detailed rejection cases.
