Shared exceptional-return shape

VS-11 probe ledger

  • Inject holder extension failure separately on normal hit, lock-free hit, and awakened waiter.
  • Inspect atomic latch tuple, global fcnt, per-thread holder, waiter grant, return, and later release.
  • Registry status: Candidate.

VS-12 probe ledger

  • Inject TDE-copy and DWB-slot failure separately.
  • Inspect DIRTY, FLUSHING, oldest-unflush LSA, waiters, retry, victim eligibility, and restart.
  • Test concurrent re-dirty separately.
  • Registry status: Candidate.
