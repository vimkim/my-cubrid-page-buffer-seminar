Replacement lab: reuse, eligibility, and cost

Compare constructed policy traces, source mechanisms, and a separately recorded native experiment.

What are we optimizing?

A bounded cache must trade page reuse against replacement-management work. Fewer page loads can explain a speedup, but cannot prove it: compare completion time for the same work and inspect read I/O separately. YCSB/sysbench results support claims about the tested workloads and conditions; effects across customer services remain unknown. Statistical design and a general measurement framework are separate work.

Interpret the counterexamples

With three frames, cyclic ABCD produces twelve LRU misses. The middle of the recency list is not a protected refuge. MRU reduces misses on that trace but loses on AB followed by repeated CD, retaining dead A/B. After the first ABCD under LRU, residents are D C B: evicting D on the next A preserves B/C. This is a local choice from that intermediate state; an MRU run from the beginning reaches a different state. D is not resident before its first load.

One move is not the whole transition

Given a BCB, zone/list lookup through flags and array indexing is O(1). Rewiring a known node is O(1). VPID lookup through a hash chain is a different operation. Promotion also acquires the LRU mutex, updates flags/counters/candidate hints with possible CAS retries, and adjusts boundaries. Adjusting k BCBs takes O(k) traversal work; this is not an elapsed-time measurement or an amortized O(1) proof. Demotion can change a zone and boundary without moving the node. Zone 3 can boost directly to the top: 3 → 2 → 1 is not a mandatory promotion sequence.

Quota is not list length

Each private protected-zone threshold is floor(quota × 0.05); quota is capped at 5000 and half the whole buffer pool. If N=4000, list length=4000 and quota=2000, full protected zones give 100/100/3800 nodes. That constructed 2.5%/2.5%/95% example is not a universal ratio. At this pin parameter tuning forces AOUT off; dormant admission branches are not active 2Q behavior. New-page final unfix normally admits at private top, while hits follow zone/age/domain conditions.

Priority, reuse, and flush are separate

Dirty and latch state are independent axes. Fix excludes frame reuse. Unfixed does not imply eligible: dirty/flushing state, waiters, flags and the protected recheck still matter. For a dirty page with no flush already running:

  • Read-latched: safe flush can proceed.
  • Write latch held by another thread: request flush; a synchronous caller waits.
  • The write holder itself: safe flush can proceed while it retains its fix.

These are source-verified conditions, not a new concurrent runtime experiment.
