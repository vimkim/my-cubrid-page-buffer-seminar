Zero-crossing rules

  1. Releasing holder makes fcnt=0.
  2. Scan under BCB protection.
  3. Skip cancelled requests.
  4. Leave FLUSH waiters.
  5. First granted WRITE: grant one and stop.
  6. First granted READ: keep scanning, leave writers/FLUSH linked, grant later readers.
  7. Keep waiter_exists if blockers remain.

Two-sided successful handoff

  1. Waker updates latch mode and global fix count.
  2. Resumed waiter records per-thread holder.
  3. Only then does caller receive ordinary successful ownership.

Fairness limits

  • Holder re-entry can pass waiters.
  • Blocking promoter inserts at head.
  • Readers can be grouped.
  • Timeout/scheduler timing is policy.

Do not claim strict FIFO or starvation freedom.
